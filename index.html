Everything you need to monitor your health

Six core tools, one simple dashboard.

Heart Rate

Track your pulse in beats per minute (BPM) at rest and during activity.

Blood Pressure

Log systolic and diastolic readings in mmHg and see how they trend.

Body Temperature

Record your temperature in °C and spot fevers early.

Blood Oxygen (SpO₂)

Keep an eye on oxygen saturation and get notified when it drops.

Blood Glucose

Note glucose levels before and after meals to understand your patterns.

History and Reports

Browse past readings and export a report to share with your doctor.
